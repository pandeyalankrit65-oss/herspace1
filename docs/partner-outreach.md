# Partner outreach: who to invite to Expert help

The Expert help directory (`/partners`) starts empty, deliberately. Organisations apply
themselves at `/partners/join`, and nobody is listed until a moderator has checked their
registration. That is what makes the directory trustworthy: a woman who sends a request is
reaching someone who agreed to receive it, and whose credentials were checked.

This page lists organisations to invite. Each is well established, works with women facing
violence, and was found in public sources in October 2026. **Confirm the contact details on the
organisation's own website before writing.** Some numbers below come from directories
and news articles, and these change.

Until partners are listed, the directory shows the free official services: 181, 112, NALSA
15100, Tele-MANAS 14416 and the NCW. Nobody hits a dead end.

## The invitation

Short and specific works best. Send it from a real person, and offer a call.

> **Subject:** Listing [organisation] in HerSpace's Expert help
>
> Dear [name],
>
> I'm [your name], building HerSpace, a free safety app for women in India: SOS alerts to
> trusted contacts, a safety map, and guidance on rights and helplines in Hindi, Tamil, Bengali,
> Marathi and English.
>
> Women using HerSpace can find checked organisations in an "Expert help" directory and send
> a request for support. We'd like to list [organisation], because of your work on [their
> focus].
>
> How it works:
> - You apply at [your site]/partners/join, with your registration (NGO Darpan ID, Bar Council
>   enrolment, RCI or medical registration). We check it before you're listed.
> - Requests reach you by email, with only what the woman chose to share and her consent. Your
>   email, phone and registration are never shown publicly.
> - You set your own fees, or none, and can pause or remove your listing at any time.
>
> Would you have 15 minutes for a call this week or next?
>
> [your name, phone]

## Approving an application (moderators)

Approve applications in `/moderation` → Partners.

- **NGOs:** look the organisation up on NGO Darpan (ngodarpan.gov.in) with its ID. Check that the
  name and address match.
- **Lawyers:** confirm the enrolment number with the State Bar Council.
- **Counsellors:** confirm psychologists' registration with the Rehabilitation Council of India
  (RCI). Counsellors without RCI registration need another check: their employer, or a
  reference from a listed NGO.
- **Doctors:** check the National Medical Register (NMC) or the state medical council.
- **Self-defence trainers:** there's no national register. Ask for the organisation behind them
  (police, school, registered academy) and a reference.
- **For every application:** call the published number, not the one on the application, and
  confirm it was really them who applied.
- **Re-checking:** an edited listing comes back to you for checking. Re-check each listing
  yearly.
